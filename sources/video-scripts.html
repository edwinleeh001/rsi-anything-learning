视频逐句讲稿与分镜Measured video scripts and storyboards

三段教学主题，各有中文与英文配音。本页逐句保留实际讲稿、实测配音区间和对应画面动作，便于复习、打印与现场讲解。画面标签为英文，烧录字幕与所选旁白语言一致。

Three teaching segments, each narrated in Mandarin and English. Every spoken sentence, measured speech interval and corresponding visual action is preserved for study, printing and presentation. Canvas labels are English; burned-in captions match the narration.

先区分教学闭环与发布评估 / Read the animation at the right scope

这是通用框架的概念教学示意。候选补丁、排序与验收结果均为示意；视频没有运行 W1、ReasonFlux 或论文基准。

官方代码复现指南说明：适配来自事先在已记录转移上进行的四阶段训练链；最终基准评估冻结权重，不执行优化器更新。因此，修复短片中“合格反馈可以更新预测器”“改变下一轮排序”的表述，解释的是框架允许的条件适配与不同检查点的作用，不能理解成发布评估在每个反馈轮后在线训练 W1。

These animations teach the general framework conceptually. Candidate patches, ranking and gate outcomes are illustrative. No W1, ReasonFlux or paper benchmark is executed.

The official code reproduction guide documents prior adaptation through a four-stage training chain over recorded transitions. Final benchmark evaluation freezes weights and performs no optimizer updates. The repair clip's statements that eligible feedback “can revise the predictor” and change later rankings explain a conditional framework route and the role of different checkpoints. They do not claim online W1 training after each feedback round in the released evaluation.

依据 / Evidence: Official code reproduction guide · 本站证据审计 / Evidence audit · Paper §2.4

时间区间精确到毫秒，来自每句实际 PCM 配音长度；区间表示说话起止，句间停顿不填入字幕。音频未做时间压缩，末句完整保留。不是逐词时间戳。

Intervals are rounded to milliseconds from measured PCM sentence lengths. They mark speech onset and end; designed gaps remain uncaptioned. Speech is not time-compressed or cut mid-sentence. These are sentence-level, not word-level timestamps.

03 · 更新不等于接受An update is not acceptance

组件分别通过，并不能直接接受组合系统。组合检查失败时，保留整对旧版本；反馈覆盖不足也不能证明模型可靠。

Separate component passes do not directly accept the assembled system. A failed pair check retains the complete old pair; insufficient feedback coverage does not establish reliability.
